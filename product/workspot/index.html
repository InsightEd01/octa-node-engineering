WorkSpot

WorkSpot is a smart attendance and workforce operations platform that uses geo-fenced check-ins, biometric verification, live monitoring, and payroll-ready exports to keep distributed teams accountable.

Get the App Structured catalog See DressCode

Core features

  • Geo-fenced attendance zones and automated check-in rules
  • Biometric verification with Face ID or fingerprint support
  • Real-time workforce monitoring and supervisor dashboards
  • Payroll-ready CSV and PDF attendance exports
  • Offline check-ins with sync when connectivity returns

Best fit

  • Operations managers overseeing field or distributed teams
  • HR teams replacing manual attendance logs
  • Supervisors who need real-time visibility into site activity
  • Organizations preparing verified attendance data for payroll
