DressCode

DressCode is a fashion social commerce platform that brings shopping, bespoke tailoring, creator monetization, direct messaging, and AI virtual try-on into one experience built for Nigeria.

Open Demo Structured catalog See Stylus AI

Core features

  • Fashion marketplace for local designers and boutiques
  • Bespoke order workflow for verified tailors
  • Built-in social feed and fashion reels
  • AI virtual try-on for outfit previews
  • Direct messaging, payments, and order tracking

Best fit

  • Fashion shoppers looking for local styles
  • Designers and boutiques selling online
  • Tailors managing bespoke orders
  • Creators monetizing style content
